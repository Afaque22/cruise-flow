# Cruise Search & Booking Platform

## Competitive Analysis

## 1. Objective

The objective of this competitive analysis is to review established cruise-booking platforms, identify common UX patterns and booking flows, and use those observations to inform the design of a modern cruise search and booking experience.

The analysis focuses on four key areas:

- Cruise search and discovery
- Search results and filtering
- Cabin selection
- Booking and checkout experience

---

## 2. Platforms Reviewed

The following established cruise-booking platforms were reviewed:

- Expedia Cruises
- CruiseDirect
- Vacations To Go

These platforms were selected because they provide established cruise-search and booking experiences with a broad range of cruise lines, itineraries, dates, and pricing options.

---

## 3. Competitive Comparison

| Area | Expedia Cruises | CruiseDirect | Vacations To Go |
|------|-----------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Cruise Search** | Search-driven discovery with multiple trip parameters | Search and browse-oriented cruise discovery | Strong emphasis on cruise search and deal discovery |
| **Filtering** | Filters help narrow available cruises | Filters and sorting for cruise options | Multiple ways to narrow cruise inventory |
| **Results** | Cruise cards with itinerary, cruise line, and pricing information | Results focused on cruise details and pricing | Results emphasize cruise details, dates, and pricing |
| **Cruise Details** | Detailed itinerary and cruise information | Detailed cruise and sailing information | Detailed sailing and itinerary information |

## 4. Key Observations

### Search & Discovery

Cruise inventory contains many variables, including departure port, destination, sailing date, cruise line, duration, and price. A useful search experience therefore needs to allow users to specify multiple parameters without making the interface difficult to understand.

**Observation:** Search should be the primary entry point into the product.

### Search Results

Users need to compare multiple cruises quickly. Important information should be visible without requiring users to open every result.

Useful information includes:

- Cruise line
- Ship
- Destination / itinerary
- Departure date
- Duration
- Starting price
- Availability or booking status

**Observation:** Results should prioritize scannability and provide filtering and sorting for larger result sets.

### Cabin Selection

Cabin selection is an important part of the cruise-booking experience. Users need to understand cabin categories, availability, and pricing before continuing with a booking.

**Observation:** Cabin selection should have a dedicated, interactive interface rather than being treated as a simple form field.

### Checkout

Booking involves several pieces of information and decisions. Presenting everything on one screen can make the process difficult to follow.

**Observation:** A progressive, multi-step checkout can separate the process into manageable stages while showing users where they are in the booking journey.

---

## 5. Product Implications

Based on the competitive review, the proposed platform will focus on four core experiences.

### 1. Multi-parameter Cruise Search

Users can search using relevant cruise parameters such as departure port, date, and cruise line.

### 2. Filterable Search Results

Results will provide clear cruise information and allow users to narrow the available options using relevant filters.

### 3. Interactive Cabin Selector

The cabin-selection experience will provide a visual representation of available, selected, and unavailable cabins while displaying relevant cabin information and pricing.

### 4. Multi-step Checkout

The booking process will be divided into clear stages so users can review their selections and provide required information progressively.

These decisions are intended to combine familiar patterns from existing cruise-booking platforms with a focused, modern booking experience.

---

## 6. Design Direction

The resulting product will prioritize:

- Clear search and discovery
- Fast comparison of cruise options
- Transparent pricing and itinerary information
- Simple cabin selection
- Progressive checkout
- Responsive design across desktop and mobile
- Consistent visual hierarchy throughout the booking journey

The goal is not to replicate any single competitor, but to use established interaction patterns as a foundation for the proposed cruise-booking experience.
